Dinner Recipes

Hearty recipes to end the day.

Spaghetti Bolognese ♡

Ingredients: spaghetti, ground beef, tomato sauce, onion, garlic

Steps:

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package.
  2. Brown the beef with onion and garlic.
  3. Stir in tomato sauce, simmer 20 minutes.
  4. Serve sauce over the spaghetti.

Result: a hearty plate of pasta in rich tomato sauce.

Grilled Salmon ♡

Ingredients: salmon fillet, olive oil, lemon, garlic, salt, pepper

Steps:

  1. Season the salmon with oil, salt, and pepper.
  2. Grill skin-side down until crisp.
  3. Flip and cook until done.
  4. Serve with a squeeze of lemon.

Result: a flaky salmon fillet with a bright lemon finish.

Grilled Steak ♡

Ingredients: steak, salt, pepper, butter, garlic, rosemary

Steps:

  1. Season the steak and bring to room temperature.
  2. Sear on a hot pan a few minutes per side.
  3. Add butter, garlic, and rosemary.
  4. Rest before slicing.

Result: a juicy seared steak with a buttery finish.

Chicken Curry ♡

Ingredients: chicken, curry powder, onion, coconut milk, rice

Steps:

  1. Cook onion until soft.
  2. Add chicken and curry powder, cook through.
  3. Stir in coconut milk, simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Serve over rice.

Result: a warm, flavorful curry served over rice.

Margherita Pizza ♡

Ingredients: pizza dough, tomato sauce, mozzarella, basil

Steps:

  1. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  2. Add mozzarella slices.
  3. Bake until the crust is golden.
  4. Top with fresh basil.

Result: a classic pizza with melted cheese and fresh basil.
