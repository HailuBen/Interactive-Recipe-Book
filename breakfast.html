Breakfast Recipes

Simple recipes to start your morning right.

Fluffy Pancakes ♡

Ingredients: flour, eggs, milk, sugar, baking powder, salt

Steps:

  1. Mix the dry ingredients together.
  2. Whisk in eggs and milk until smooth.
  3. Pour batter onto a hot buttered pan.
  4. Flip when bubbles form, cook until golden.

Result: a tall stack of soft golden pancakes.

Avocado Toast ♡

Ingredients: bread, avocado, salt, pepper, chili flakes

Steps:

  1. Toast the bread until crisp.
  2. Mash the avocado with salt and pepper.
  3. Spread avocado on the toast.
  4. Top with chili flakes.

Result: crisp toast topped with creamy avocado.

Cheese Omelette ♡

Ingredients: eggs, shredded cheese, salt, pepper, butter

Steps:

  1. Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper.
  2. Melt butter in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour in the eggs and let them set.
  4. Add cheese, fold, and cook until melted.

Result: a soft folded omelette with melted cheese.

French Toast ♡

Ingredients: bread, eggs, milk, cinnamon, syrup

Steps:

  1. Whisk eggs, milk, and cinnamon together.
  2. Dip each bread slice in the mixture.
  3. Cook on a buttered pan until golden on both sides.
  4. Serve warm with syrup.

Result: golden, soft french toast with a sweet syrup finish.

Waffles ♡

Ingredients: flour, eggs, milk, butter, baking powder

Steps:

  1. Mix the batter until smooth.
  2. Pour batter into a hot waffle iron.
  3. Cook until golden and crisp.
  4. Top with fruit or syrup.

Result: crisp golden waffles with a soft center.
