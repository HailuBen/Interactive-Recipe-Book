Lunch Recipes

Easy recipes for the middle of the day.

Caesar Salad ♡

Ingredients: romaine lettuce, parmesan, croutons, caesar dressing

Steps:

  1. Chop the romaine lettuce.
  2. Toss with caesar dressing.
  3. Add croutons and parmesan.
  4. Finish with black pepper.

Result: a crisp, creamy salad with crunchy croutons.

Chicken Salad Sandwich ♡

Ingredients: cooked chicken, mayonnaise, celery, lettuce, bread

Steps:

  1. Chop the cooked chicken.
  2. Mix chicken with mayonnaise and celery.
  3. Layer lettuce and chicken salad on bread.
  4. Slice in half and serve.

Result: a creamy chicken sandwich with a fresh crunch.

Tomato Soup ♡

Ingredients: tomatoes, onion, garlic, broth, cream

Steps:

  1. Saute onion and garlic until soft.
  2. Add tomatoes and broth, simmer 15 minutes.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Stir in cream and serve warm.

Result: a warm, velvety bowl of tomato soup.

Greek Salad ♡

Ingredients: tomato, cucumber, olives, feta cheese, olive oil

Steps:

  1. Chop the tomato and cucumber.
  2. Add olives and feta cheese.
  3. Drizzle with olive oil.
  4. Toss gently and serve.

Result: a fresh, tangy salad with salty feta.

Grilled Cheese ♡

Ingredients: bread, cheese slices, butter

Steps:

  1. Butter one side of each bread slice.
  2. Place cheese between the unbuttered sides.
  3. Grill on a pan until golden on both sides.
  4. Slice and serve warm.

Result: a crispy sandwich with melted cheese inside.
